🎶 LiveDJ

LiveDJ is a cross-platform application that empowers DJs to stream live sets, share their music with fans worldwide, 
and connect with their audience in real time. Fans can tune in, interact through comments, and book live performances directly within the app.

✨ Features

Live Streaming – DJs can broadcast sets globally.

Interactive Fan Engagement – Real-time comments and reactions during live shows.

DJ Profiles – Customizable profiles with bio, photos, genres, and portfolio links.

Booking System – Fans can reserve time slots and book DJs for events.

Secure Payments – Integrated options for booking and reservations.

Cross-Platform Support – Works seamlessly across mobile (iOS/Android) and desktop.
